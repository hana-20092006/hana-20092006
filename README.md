## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🌧️ [MUNSOON](https://github.com/hana-20092006/Munsoon)
**AI-Powered Energy Resilience for Monsoon-Prone Solar Homes**

Predicts solar availability and household energy-shortage risk during prolonged monsoons, combining machine learning, energy simulation, RAG, and agentic recommendations.

- End-to-end application with a React frontend and FastAPI backend
- Explainable recommendations for battery conservation and essential-load prioritization
- Energy-resilience improvements evaluated through controlled simulations

🔗 [Live Demo](https://munsoon.onrender.com/)

### 🔐 [SentinelVault](https://github.com/hana-20092006/sentinel-vault-mic)
**TinyML-Powered Transaction Security**

Combines contextual financial-risk scoring, an on-device TinyML anomaly detector, and cryptographically verified transaction approval.

🏆 **Winner, Finance Track, Recursion II Hackathon**

### 🎬 [Movie Recommendation System](https://github.com/hana-20092006/movie-recommender)
**Hybrid Recommendation Engine**

Combines collaborative filtering, SVD, and content-based recommendations on the MovieLens 100K dataset.

- Evaluated with an **RMSE of 0.9355**
- Containerized with Docker and deployed on Hugging Face Spaces

🔗 [Try the Live Demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Philips20/movie-recommender)

### 🌍 [Study Abroad Guide](https://github.com/hana-20092006/study-abroad-guide)
**A Platform for International Education Planning**

A full-stack web application that helps students explore study-abroad opportunities and access relevant guidance.

🔗 [Live Demo](https://study-abroad-guide-1.onrender.com/)

### 📦 [Agentic Supply Chain Guardian](https://github.com/Soumadip-Eagle123/agentic-supply-chain-guardian)
An agentic logistics platform that identifies high-risk shipments and generates mitigation recommendations.

🚀 Selected for the VIT internal round of Smart India Hackathon 2026.

### 🚦 [Early Congestion Prediction and Adaptive Routing](https://github.com/hana-20092006/Early-Congestion-Prediction-and-Adaptive-Routing)
**AI-Powered Traffic Congestion Prediction and Adaptive Routing**

An intelligent transportation project focused on predicting traffic congestion early and adapting routes to improve traffic flow, with consideration for climate-related stressors.

- Explores machine learning for early congestion prediction
- Combines congestion forecasting with simulation-based adaptive routing
- Applies AI to transportation resilience and real-time decision-making

🔗 [Live Demo](https://srijani-das07.github.io/Early-Congestion-Prediction-and-Adaptive-Routing/)
